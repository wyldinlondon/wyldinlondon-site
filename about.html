About WYLD in London

WYLD in London began as a father-son journey to explore the best pubs in London and share honest, unbiased opinions.

We personally visit every location. We pay for what we order. We give our genuine verdict.

Now expanding into London cafés, our mission remains the same — discover the best and tell it how it is.
